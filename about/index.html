About ONEWORK

As experts in digital twins, we leverage systems engineering, IoT technologies, data analytics, cloud computing, and augmented reality to map the entire product lifecycle (BLM) of real-world assets—such as buildings and systems—into a virtual BIM environment. By consolidating complex, multi-source data and presenting it through 3D technologies, we deliver unprecedented levels of information visibility and insight to our users.

Carl Yu

Founder / Tech-Lead

Graduated from Massachusetts Institute of Technology;

Building Computational BIM Specialist

Carl Yu holds a Master's degree from MIT, majoring in Design and Computation, class of 2009. Past experience includes ARUP engineering company as team leader, and ATG (Advanced Technology Group) as BIM manager that led Shenzhen office's strategy team for project implementation across the globe. Carl is also an expert in parametric design for multidisciplinary design, such as design analysis and conducting training courses for ARUP East Asia. During his time at MIT, Carl worked as a full-time researcher at the MIT Mobile Experience Lab, where he designed systems that radically reinvent connections between people. Carl's main area of expertise focuses on integrating technology into material and physical objects: Sustainable Technology, Parametric Design, Simulation Tools, Physical Feedback Loop System Design, and Mobile Device. While an experienced architect, he's also equipped with skills in Architectural Representation, Concept Design, User Experience Study, HCI, and Behavior Modeling.

OUR STORY

2016

Digital twins for the AEC industry, since 2016.

Founded in 2016, Onework is dedicated to building digital twins for the AEC industry. Our core business integrates IoT and BIM with big data analytics, supported by our self-developed 3D data management platform. It handles large-scale, multi-category spatial data at once — cleansing and converting it into a seamless fusion of physical and digital urban environments.

We provide regional operators with operational data management, governments with spatial data visualisation and regulatory tools, and visitors with integrated virtual-real mobility applications. Our key clients include international data centres, airports and large campuses — and we are building a suite of tools that brings order to the fragmented information created by industry specialisation.
