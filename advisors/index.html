ADVISORS

Guided by leaders in semiconductors, nanotech and smart mobility

  • Dr. Kang L. Wang

    Academician, Academia Sinica · MIT, UCLA

    Five decades leading nanotechnology, semiconductor and interdisciplinary research, including institutes in green nanotechnology and quantum science. He guides the R&D vision and high-tech systems integration behind our building management products.

  • Dr. Mau-Chung Frank Chang

    Wintek Chair Professor of Electrical Engineering, UCLA

    A leader in high-speed, high-frequency semiconductor systems, from GaAs HBT/BiFET technology to reconfigurable mm-wave SoCs. Member of the U.S. National Academy of Engineering and Academia Sinica, and IEEE David Sarnoff Award recipient. He advises on smart sensor hardware, power-efficient communication modules and scalable building management.
